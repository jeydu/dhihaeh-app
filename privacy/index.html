Dhihaeh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (online play and voice messages)

Dhihaeh is a card game you play on your iPhone — against computer players, or online with friends in a private room.

What we collect

Nothing is collected or stored about you.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Offline games never use the internet.

Online play

When you play online, the app connects to the Dhihaeh room server and sends:

  • the display name you type (any nickname you like), so the other players in your room can see who is who;
  • your moves (the cards you play) and a random room token, so the game can run and you can rejoin your seat if your connection drops.

Voice messages (push-to-talk). Only while you hold the talk button, the app records a short clip (at most 10 seconds) and sends it through the server to the other players in your room. The server passes it straight on; it does not store, log or listen to it. The other players' phones play it and then delete it.

The server keeps this only in memory while your room is open. It is not saved to a database, not written to logs, not shared with anyone outside your room, and not used for anything else. When the room closes or the server restarts, it is gone. The server is hosted by Render (render.com).

What stays on your device

Your current game and your running score are saved on your iPhone so you can continue later. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Dhihaeh is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything about what the app sends changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
