Dhihaeh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Dhihaeh is a card game you play on your iPhone against computer players.

What we collect

Nothing. Dhihaeh does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not use the internet in this version.

What stays on your device

Your current game and your running score are saved on your iPhone so you can continue later. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Dhihaeh is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and will explain exactly what is sent and why.
