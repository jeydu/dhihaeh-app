Dhihaeh – Card Game
Support

Dhihaeh is the Maldivian card game of the tens, for iPhone. You play with a partner against two opponents — computer players offline, or friends online in a private room (share the room code).

Need help or found a problem?
Email jadhullo7@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you're using.

How to play

  • The player on the shuffler's right gets the first card and leads. Play goes anticlockwise.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick, unless Hukum is played.
  • Hukum starts undecided. The first player who can't follow suit decides it: the suit of the card they play becomes Hukum at once.
  • Each trick won is a trick point. Capture three of the four 10s to win the hand. If the 10s split 2–2, the first team to 7 trick points wins.
  • Tap a card to lift it, tap it again to play it. On your last card of a suit you can use Hard Throw to tell your partner you're out.

Your game

Your game is saved on your iPhone after every card, so you can close the app and carry on later. Deleting the app deletes it.
